Magic ePaper

Design and transfer content to NFC ePaper badges — wirelessly, battery-free.

Download APK View Source

Features

✏️

Canvas Editor

Free-form drawing with layers, text, emoji, and font customization. Designs save as editable JSON to your library.

🖼️

Image Import

Import any image and apply ePaper-optimized processing: Floyd–Steinberg and Atkinson dithering, brightness, contrast, and color filters.

📇

Card Templates

One-tap templates for Employee ID, Price Tag, Business Card, Calendar, Restaurant Menu, Weather Snapshot, and more.

📱

NFC Transfer

Write images and NDEF records (text, URLs, vCards, app launch intents) to ePaper badges over NFC in seconds.

📚

Image Library

Processed images are saved locally for instant re-transfer without redesigning. Editable designs can be reopened and modified.

🔳

QR & Barcodes

Generate QR codes, Code 128, EAN, and other barcode formats directly on-badge — ideal for product or event labels.

📦

Bulk Import

Upload a CSV to generate many unique cards at once — all four template types supported, output goes straight to the library.

🔧

Command Console

Raw hex / APDU terminal for power users and hardware developers to probe and debug NFC ePaper tags directly.

Quick Start

1

Install the app

Download the latest APK from the app branch and sideload it on your Android device. Enable NFC in system settings.

2

Select your display

Open the app and tap Displays to pick your ePaper model. The app will configure the correct resolution and color palette automatically.

3

Create your design

Use the Canvas for free-form drawing, import an image via the editor, or pick a ready-made Template (Employee ID, Calendar, Weather, etc.).

4

Transfer to badge

Tap Write NFC, hold your phone against the badge, and the image is transferred wirelessly. No battery required on the badge.

5

Manage your library

Every processed image is saved to the Library. Tap any item to re-transfer instantly, or tap Edit to reopen an editable design.
